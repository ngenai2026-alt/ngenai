Building Next Generation AI for Enterprise

Next Generation AI (NgenAI) is an AI development studio based in Hinjewadi, Pune. We focus on building advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Agentic AI systems for modern enterprises.

We help organizations transform their internal data into intelligent, action-driven solutions that improve efficiency, decision-making, and scalability.

Our expertise lies in designing custom AI agents, enterprise knowledge copilots, and workflow automation systems powered by robust RAG architectures.
